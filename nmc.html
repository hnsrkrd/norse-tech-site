NORSE MINING CENTER · NMC

WHERE MINING BECOMES MEASURABLE.

Norse Mining Center, NMC, is a testing, training and process development centre under establishment within the NORSE system.

WHAT NMC DOES

TEST. DEVELOP. TRAIN. DOCUMENT.

NMC is the NORSE centre for ore testwork, process development, operator competence and technical documentation. It supports the decisions described under Process without duplicating the role of NMT or the Tanzania implementation entities.

TESTWORK

Characterisation & Testing

Representative samples are examined and tested to establish the technical basis for further process work.

PROCESS DEVELOPMENT

From results to testable routes

NMC helps turn test results into practical process configurations that can be compared before a route is selected.

PEOPLE

Training & Competence

Operator training and process understanding support safer, more consistent and repeatable operation.

DOCUMENTATION

Verification & Records

Sampling, test results and process documentation create a technical record that can be reviewed and followed up.

UNDER ESTABLISHMENT

Norse Mining Center, NMC, is currently under establishment. Activities will expand as the centre becomes operational.

COLLABORATION WITH NiT

Norse in Tanzania, NiT, collaborates with NMC in Tanzania while remaining a separate actor.
