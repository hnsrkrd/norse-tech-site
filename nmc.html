NORSE MINING CENTER · NMC

WHERE TECHNOLOGY BECOMES KNOWLEDGE.

Norse Mining Center, NMC, is being developed as the place where technology becomes knowledge — and where knowledge is translated into practical mineral processing and production.

THE SHARED TECHNICAL PLATFORM

BUILD COMPETENCE ONCE. MAKE IT AVAILABLE TO MANY.

NMC is intended to give PML holders, their personnel and their TSA partners access to a professional technical environment for training, testing and mineral processing.

Instead of every new mining operation having to build the same competence, equipment and organisation from the beginning, several independent operations can use one shared professional platform.

The objective is higher productivity, better process control, stronger local competence and practical transfer of technical knowledge to Tanzania.

TECHNICAL SUPPORT SHOULD BUILD CAPACITY — NOT CREATE DEPENDENCE.

NMC is not designed to replace the mine. It is designed to build the competence that allows the mine and its people to perform better.

WHAT NMC DOES

TRAIN. TEST. PROCESS. DOCUMENT.

The centre brings learning, testwork and practical process operation together in the same technical environment.

TRAIN

Equipment & Process Competence

Operators and PML personnel learn how individual machines work, how the stages connect and how a complete mineral-processing line is operated and controlled.

TEST

Ore & Process Evaluation

Representative material is characterised and tested so that process decisions are based on the ore and measured results rather than assumptions.

PROCESS

A Real Technical Environment

Crushing, grinding, classification and physical separation can be demonstrated and operated as connected process stages, subject to the licences and approvals required in Tanzania.

DOCUMENT

Measurement & Verification

Sampling, assays, operating data and mass balance create records that make the process easier to understand, review and improve.

FROM MACHINE TO SYSTEM

YOU CANNOT TEACH A PROCESS LINE ON PAPER.

Crushing, grinding, classification and separation are not only production stages at NMC. They are part of the training environment. People need to see how material moves, how settings affect the next stage and how the complete line behaves under real operating conditions.

Understand the machine → Understand the process → Operate the line → Build local competence

WHY A SHARED CENTER?

ONE PROFESSIONAL ENVIRONMENT. SEVERAL INDEPENDENT OPERATIONS.

PML HOLDERS

Access without rebuilding everything.

Participating licence holders can access technical competence, process knowledge and a structured operating environment without each operation having to duplicate the same capability from the beginning.

TSA DELIVERY

A place for practical knowledge transfer.

TSA support can include more than equipment. NMC provides a setting where technical methods can be demonstrated, practised and progressively transferred to Tanzanian personnel.

TANZANIA

Competence that stays.

The long-term value is not only the equipment installed. It is the ability of local personnel to understand, operate and improve the process independently.

UNDER ESTABLISHMENT

Norse Mining Center, NMC, is currently under establishment. The functions described on this page represent the planned NMC operating model and will expand as the centre becomes operational and the required Tanzanian licences and approvals are in place.

COLLABORATION WITH NiT

Norse in Tanzania, NiT, collaborates with NMC in Tanzania while remaining a separate actor.
