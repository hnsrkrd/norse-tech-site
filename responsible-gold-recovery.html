Gravity vs Mercury & Cyanide

NO MERCURY IN THE CLEAN PROCESS

CLEAN MATERIAL STAYS CLEAN.

Mercury is not part of the NORSE clean processing line. Material with a known or suspected history of mercury use must be identified and kept separate from clean ore and concentrates.

HISTORICALLY CONTAMINATED MATERIAL

Material with a known or suspected mercury history is kept outside the clean-material route until it has been separately identified, evaluated and given an appropriate handling plan.

MEASURABLE RESULTS

MAKE THE RESULT REVIEWABLE.

Where a commercial service model applies, measurement and documentation should make it possible to review what entered the process, what was recovered and what remained.

WHEN GRAVITY IS NOT ENOUGH

ANALYSE WHAT REMAINS.

Some ores contain gold that cannot be recovered effectively by gravity alone. The remaining material must be analysed before an additional processing route is selected.
