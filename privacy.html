Why We Process Data

Information is used to receive and respond to enquiries, perform an initial technical review of submitted material and evaluate appropriate next steps.

Legal basis: legitimate interests, subject to the required balancing assessment.

Data Collected

Name, organisation, country, email address, mining area, material information, mercury-history information, free text and voluntarily uploaded technical files.

Retention

If an enquiry does not proceed, the information will normally be deleted within 12 months of the last contact. If it becomes part of a project or contract, relevant information may be retained under the applicable project/legal retention rules.

Your Rights

Applicable rights may include access, correction, deletion, restriction and objection. Complaints may be submitted to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).

Service Providers & International Transfers

Hosting, email and form providers may act as processors. Where personal data is transferred outside the EU/EEA, appropriate safeguards will be applied where required.

Cookies & Tracking

The first launch is designed without non-essential tracking. If analytics, advertising pixels, embedded third-party media or other non-essential tracking is introduced later, privacy and cookie controls must be reviewed before activation.
