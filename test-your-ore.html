WHAT TYPE OF ORE DO YOU HAVE?

Start with the material. Tell us what you know. You do not need to have all the answers.

Please do not upload passports, identity documents, financial information or sensitive personal information.

You may attach photographs, assay results, laboratory reports or other relevant technical information.

Privacy

The information you submit is used by Norse Holding to review and respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, evaluate appropriate next steps. If your enquiry does not result in further activity, the information will normally be deleted within 12 months of the last contact.

By submitting this form, you acknowledge that you have read our Privacy Policy.

Submitting information does not constitute an acceptance of material or a commitment to process it. All material must be evaluated before a processing route can be recommended.

Your email application should now open. The enquiry is addressed to Test-ore@Norse-Tech.se. Please attach any selected files manually before sending.

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT?

01 — INITIAL REVIEW
We review the information you have provided.
02 — SAMPLE REQUIREMENTS
If appropriate, we agree how a representative sample should be submitted.
03 — TESTING
The material is characterized and tested.
04 — PROCESS EVALUATION
The technically appropriate recovery route is evaluated.
05 — NEXT STEP
This may include further testing, pilot processing or process design.
