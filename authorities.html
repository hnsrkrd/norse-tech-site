THE AUTHORITY VIEW

FROM ORE ORIGIN TO VERIFIED RESULT.

The authority route brings the control points forward: where the material came from, what entered the process, what was recovered, what left as tailings, and how the figures are documented.

01 · Origin

Traceability

Material is linked to its source and owner before processing, creating a documented starting point.

02 · Intake

Weighing

Incoming material is weighed and registered before processing begins.

03 · Evidence

Sampling

Representative and continuous sampling creates evidence for grade, performance and settlement.

04 · Recovery

Physical First

Gravity-based recovery is used where the mineralogy supports it, avoiding mercury and cyanide in the NORSE gravity route.

05 · Control

Mass Balance

Input, concentrate and tailings are measured so that recovery and losses can be checked against the material balance.

06 · Output

Verification

The recovered gold, process records and settlement basis can be documented rather than treated as a black box.

WHY THIS MATTERS

FORMALISATION ONLY WORKS IF THE FORMAL ROUTE IS CREDIBLE.

A system that is easier to inspect, measure and explain gives miners, operators and authorities a shared factual basis.
