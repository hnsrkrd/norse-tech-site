YOUR ORE

EVERY ORE IS DIFFERENT.

How the gold occurs in the ore determines what has to happen next. NORSE begins with the material.

Gravity potential: High

Gold already liberated by nature and occurring in gravel, sand and sediments.

Typically starts with physical sizing and gravity potential.

Core application

Gold that can be physically liberated from hard rock through controlled crushing and grinding.

Requires controlled liberation before recovery can be evaluated.

Test required

Gold occurring together with sulphide minerals. Recoverability depends on how the gold occurs and how effectively it can be liberated.

Requires testwork to understand liberation and recovery potential.

Specialist evaluation

Very fine gold locked inside mineral structures and not necessarily recoverable by physical separation alone.

Requires specialist evaluation before a process route is selected.

THE FIRST QUESTION

CAN THE GOLD BE PHYSICALLY LIBERATED?

YES

Free gold

Where gold is sufficiently liberated, gravity separation may provide an effective recovery route.

PARTLY

Mixed liberation

Some gold may be free while some remains associated with other minerals. Testwork determines the route.

NOT YET

Locked gold

When gold remains locked in mineral structures, additional evaluation is required before selecting a next step.
